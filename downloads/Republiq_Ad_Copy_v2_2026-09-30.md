## Notes before these go live (not part of the copy)

1. **Hook 6_v2's answer to "why are you selling it" is my wording.** It's built from facts in the videos (120+ markets, we get paid to build, the partner owns it). Check that it's the answer Eric really gives on calls, and swap in the real reason if it's different.
2. **The imagined quote in Hook 2_v2** ("I have yet to meet a single professional who said…") is written so it clearly reads as Eric paraphrasing, not as a testimonial. Keep it that way. Don't turn it into a quote card with a name.
3. **Standalone_v2 carries earnings figures.** In the US, earnings claims for a business opportunity usually need written records behind them and can't imply typical results, and Meta reviews income claims closely. I've added an honest "not everyone's first deal looks like this" line and "Individual results vary" in the description. Have compliance check the deal records and the wording before this one runs.
4. **Everything else is taken from the videos:** 60 days, "performs or we work for free", 120+ partners, two and a half years, 98% retention, 25 to 50-plus verified buyers, four to eight hours "on average", and the build list. The terms of the guarantee should be spelled out on the booking page.
5. **Headlines are 34 to 48 characters.** Meta cuts headlines off at different lengths depending on placement, so check the preview in Ads Manager.
