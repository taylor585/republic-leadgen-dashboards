## Script 1: "The question on every call"
*Angle: the prospect's bluntest objection, asked in their own words and answered honestly, including what's in it for us.* · 360 words, about 2 min 35 s

When we hop on a call with a prospect, there's one question that comes up almost every single time, usually about ten minutes in, and it goes something like this: "If this AI lead generation thing works so well, why on earth are you selling it instead of running it for yourselves?"

It's a fair question, and if I were sitting where you are, I'd be asking it too.

The short answer is: we're builders, and a lead generation company is only ever as good as the person who owns the buyer relationships in that market. We can't be the local owner in 120 different markets at once, and we'd do a pretty poor job of it if we tried. What we can do is build the whole machine properly, over and over again, and get paid for building it. That's our business. Owning it and running it in your market is yours.

So here's what that actually looks like. We build you a completely automated AI lead generation company in 60 days: your brand, your website, your landing pages, your CRM, your automations, your AI voice agent, your AI sales rep and your speed-to-lead systems, all under your name and owned by you from day one. Then we bring you the buyers, which is the part everybody else skips. You get a verified list of 25 to 50-plus businesses in your market that already pay for leads every single week, loaded into your outreach system, and we work closely with you to land your first paying client in just weeks.

We've been doing this for two and a half years now. We've built more than 120 of these partner companies, and 98% of those partners are still working with us today. That's not a number I read in a report. Those are the people we're on the phone with every week.

And it performs or we work for free.

So if you've got the same question, good. Click the button below, book your free consultation, and ask me it directly. I'll give you the whole answer, and then we'll work out together whether this is actually right for you.

## Claim and production notes (not for recording)

1. **Script 1's reason for selling instead of running it is my draft.** I built it from facts in v2 (120+ markets, we get paid to build, the partner owns it). Before recording, check that it's the answer Eric really gives on calls. If the real answer is the "middle part of the market" reason from your earlier script 6, use that instead. The honest reason only works if it's the true one.
2. **The imagined lines have to sound imagined.** "I have yet to meet a single one who said…" and the objection questions are written so the listener can hear they're Eric paraphrasing, not quoting someone. Don't put them on screen as quote cards with a name or a face.
3. **Earnings claims (Script 4).** The Richard and Vivek figures come from Standalone_v2. In the US, earnings claims for a business opportunity usually need written substantiation and must not imply they're typical results, and Meta reviews income claims closely. I've added a spoken "not everyone's first deal looks like this" line. Have whoever handles compliance check the deal records and the wording before Script 4 runs. The other four scripts contain no earnings figures.
4. **Kept exactly as in v2:** 60 days, "performs or we work for free", 120+ partner companies, two and a half years, 98% retention, 25 to 50-plus verified buyers, four to eight hours "on average", and the full build list. The terms of the guarantee need to be spelled out on the booking page and on the call.
5. **"Eighteen months" and "thirty people" (Script 3)** come from v2. They're dated to when you recorded, so check they still hold before you reuse them later.
6. **Speed-to-lead wording.** I said the systems "pick up every lead and qualify it". I avoided "within seconds" because v2 never claims a response time.

## Test plan

- Run these as five new concepts against the best current v2 hook, with the body left as each script has it. The concepts differ by argument, so they give you real information about what works, not just about wording.
- Judge them on booked calls, show rate and qualified-call rate from the call team, not on clicks or hook rate alone. An objection-led script can get fewer clicks and still produce better calls.
- Give each concept enough spend to reach a meaningful number of bookings before you cut it. Record the market and audience with each result, so a winner isn't assumed to carry over to a different audience.
