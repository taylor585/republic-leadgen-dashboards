Lead Tiers Research · mechanics, costs & test protocols

Three tiers of intent. Price follows proof.

Tier 1 guesses intent from behaviour. Tier 2 reads intent the prospect stated in their own words. Tier 3 has intent proven by an action taken in our funnel. This dashboard holds the mechanics of each tier and the exact tests that turn claims into numbers.

TIER 2

LeadHunter — the core engine

Mechanics — what we know

  • Behavioural intent mining: finds people saying they urgently need the service — statements in their own words, not inferred clicks
  • 7-day recency window (30-day comparison pass in the battery)
  • 0–100 scoring: 70+ = sellable/guarantee-grade · 80+ = consistently qualified
  • Self-learning: stores winning keyword patterns, retries winners, spends a few exploratory searches per run, builds its own database — volume should improve over time
  • Enriched output: contactability fields (phone/email/LinkedIn); completeness measured in the battery
  • Provenance: Taylor lands clients with it personally, without ads — the strongest proof of concept we have
CLAIMS THAT NEED A FILE BEHIND THEM (A5)

The ~91% match rate and the 10 → 5–7 conversations → ~2 deals pipeline will become the most-quoted stats in every sales call. Before a rep says them once: which verticals, what sample size, where does the data live? If the run says a different number, the different number wins.

Guarantee-backing numbers get set at the worst quartile, never the average.

TIER 3

The Meta ads siphon — ultra-high intent

The flow (to build in WS-1)

Meta ad → funnel → qualification form → booking action (call scheduled within ~2–3 days) → GHL automation tags the contact as Tier-3 inventory → routed to the buyer's pipeline (basic) or scheduled into their calendar (premium). Works hand-in-hand with the fresh-pixel architecture: DQ leads never touch the pixel or Tier-3 inventory.

CROSS-TIER

Delivery, pricing & dependencies

Service levels & routing

Basic: hard-rule qualification against buyer parameters → instant delivery to buyer pipeline + their own system. SLA: minutes.
Premium: qualification + appointment scheduling (human setter until compliance clears AI) → delivered at "scheduled" stage, priced higher.
Routing: round-robin across active matching buyers; the active/inactive kill-switch stops routing instantly.

Dependency notes

  • Territory (D1) changes every per-vertical number — decide before locking any table
  • The A4 spend table ($1k/$2.5k/$5k CPL) is Meta-campaign data, not LeadHunter — it needs the fresh pixel live
  • Compliance (D13) can delete the AI option from premium scheduling — price premium assuming a human until proven otherwise
