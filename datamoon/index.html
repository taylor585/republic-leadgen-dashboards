THE POINT

DataMoon's data alone is thin (behavioural, low-intent). Its superpower is the LinkedIn URL on every row. This skill exploits exactly that — it converts "here are 36 accountants" into "here is precisely how to advertise to accountants" — which is what makes the Tier-1 audience actually worth uploading to Meta.

HOW IT WORKED

Exactly how we pulled the posts

The mechanism, step by step

  1. Parsed the CSV — kept only professional fields (name, title, firm, city), stripped every address/phone/email/income, and flagged rows where the LinkedIn URL didn't match the name.
  2. Picked the five most likely to post (partners, CIO, marketing) — because in this segment the leaders post and the staff don't.
  3. Ran Google advanced-search dorks per person — e.g. site:linkedin.com/posts "Nick Cadden" and site:linkedin.com/in "Nick Cadden" Birmingham AL.
  4. Read the results from Google's index — the post URL and snippet reveal each post's topic without ever opening LinkedIn.
  5. Blended posts (voice) with public profile/press (coverage) for anyone who doesn't post.
  6. Aggregated the five into one fingerprint → wrote the creative brief → discarded the raw.

The two findings that shaped the design

DIRECT LINKEDIN IS BLOCKED — HTTP 999

Every attempt to fetch a LinkedIn profile or post directly returned LinkedIn's block code. Google's index is the only viable read path — your instinct to go through Google wasn't just safest, it's the only thing that works.

DATAMOON ROWS AREN'T FACTS

~1 in 6 rows had a name↔profile or location mismatch (a "Sophia Archer" row was actually Steve Rutland; a "Birmingham AL" contact was in Florida). The parser flags these; they get verified or dropped before any Meta upload.

LIVE SYSTEM

How it runs live — batches of 50

The pilot proves it on a handful. Live, you run any audience in batches of 50, aggregating after each and stopping when the picture stops changing.

The loop

parse_audience.py  → contacts.json   (once; flags mismatches)

for each batch of 50:
   sample.py --n 50 --mode mixed --seed 
   dork.py --from-sample batch.json
   → read public posts+profiles via Google
   aggregate.py → batch_fingerprint.json
   merge into running fingerprint
   if themes stable for 1–2 batches → STOP

discard raw · keep fingerprint + report

Why 50, and what each batch costs

  • Confidence: you never need everyone. A random 250 (five batches) reads a 10,000-person audience to ±6% at 95%. One batch already gives a solid directional fingerprint.
  • Pacing: 50 is comfortable for the Google reads without tripping bot-blocks — space queries out; stay well under ~50 searches/hour if using a real browser.
  • Per batch: ~50 profiles found, ~18–20 with readable posts, minutes of wall-clock on the Google path, near-zero cost.
  • Stopping rule: when a new batch adds no new themes or vocabulary, the segment is saturated — stop and save the credits.

Knob · more voice

--mode likely-posters or raise N — when you want vocabulary and tone.

Knob · more representative

--mode random — when you want true demographics, not just the vocal minority.

Knob · quotable numbers

Run batches until themes stabilise (~N250) — when the brief needs hard percentages.

WHAT'S STRONG

Coverage is near-total — we can characterise everyone from public profile signal, and add real voice from the ~40% who post. No LinkedIn risk, no message sent, no PII touched. The output is a genuine creative brief, not a data dump. And it's now a repeatable skill, not a one-off.

WHAT TO KNOW HONESTLY

Post depth depends on the role mix — quiet segments (junior, non-tech) yield fewer posts, so lean on profile signal there. Small samples are directional hypotheses, not precise averages — widen N for quotable numbers. And DataMoon's own accuracy is the weak link: verify rows before trusting or uploading.

GUARDRAILS

Baked into the skill

META UPLOAD GATE

Meta requires you warrant "all necessary rights and permissions and a lawful basis" for uploaded data. The skill never uploads — that's a separate human step after verifying non-clean rows. Protects the ad account.

NO LINKEDIN LOGIN

Reads Google's public index only. Never automates against LinkedIn (that's what got Proxycurl shut down). Reading public data is defensible; the ban risk is avoided.

PII MINIMISED

Addresses, phones, personal emails, income and net-worth are stripped at parse time and never used. Sample → aggregate → discard the raw. No dossier lake.
